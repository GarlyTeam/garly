# City Studio, missions and community rewards

Product overview reviewed 19 November 2026. This is the optional **website** community programme, separate from the free personal safety app. See the [whitepaper v1.4](Garly-Whitepaper.pdf), [City Ambassadors](https://garlyapp.pro/app/ambassadors.html) and the programme's in-product requirements.

## Roles and membership

- A Citizen can join a city without buying tokens or entering a payout wallet. Authentication, location/invitation eligibility and move restrictions still apply.
- A valid saved Solana payout address is required to receive eligible token payments. A connected payment wallet is not automatically the payout destination.
- An Ambassador acquires an available title with $GARLY. It is a role inside the programme, not ownership of a real city, property or an NFT.
- Buying a title is a payment to the programme vault, not staking. Selling requires a buyer; a displayed asking price is not guaranteed market value. Releasing the title does not refund the original payment.
- Ambassador Premium is the separate optional 1,000,000 $GARLY payment described by the programme. It is not Garly Pro or store subscription revenue.

The older [programme manual](https://garlyapp.pro/app/ambassadors/user-manual/) remains useful for its detailed original rules, but its wallet-required joining text predates the confirmed wallet-optional update. Do not use an older sentence to reintroduce that requirement.

## What City Studio adds

| Space | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Visual city | Explore the city's access points and visual worlds. Changing a visual world does not change membership or reset city progress. |
| Piazza | Browse initiatives first, read their details and use creation/management tools according to the account's role. |
| Creative tools and Kit | Prepare and export graphics using the available templates and supported custom artwork flow. A download alone is not proof of a social publication. |
| Mission diary | Review requirements, check results, credited XP and the social archive. Pending, technically matched, declared and credited states are different. |
| Whistle | Use the embedded external AI workspace and eligible Ambassador ticket. |

Access follows the authenticated account and its role. City tools are not permission to edit another person's city. Private tests must not be reported as campaign conversions.

Referral dashboards distinguish visits, sessions with clicks, registration stages, completed registrations and attributed first openings where available. A store-button click is not a confirmed download; an attributed first opening is not a complete count of all store installations.

## Configured new mission weights

| Mission | Configured weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Qualified retained invitation | 12 XP |
| Social content mission | Up to 8 XP |
| Qualified artwork reuse | 6 XP |
| Qualified Piazza engagement | 4 XP |

These are configured Studio weights. A mission must be enabled for the account and meet its actual requirements and cadence. Existing missions retain their own rules. The shared daily mission cap is 20 XP; the remaining room can reduce a credit. Do not describe posting an initiative as automatically satisfying a participation requirement.

The social allowance is **once per UTC week across X, Instagram and TikTok together**, not an allowance per platform. Duplicate and reuse controls apply. A technical match alone is not an award, and an XP award is not an immediate fixed $GARLY payment.

## Social evidence

Instagram/TikTok content checks can compare an author, fresh code, required text and eligible image or video evidence. Publication or an eligible update with a fresh code can qualify. Supported caption edits do not by themselves invalidate an otherwise valid code. Public source data may be temporarily unavailable.

The X declaration route can record a submitted post and grant the permitted contribution without calling X. It remains a **creator declaration**, not automatic proof of publication, authorship or a native partnership label. Optional human review is a separate event; do not claim that every entry has been reviewed.

A native disclosure label is not a universal technical prerequisite in the current content-check mechanism. This is not certification that a platform approved the post or that disclosure requirements have been satisfied. General raid payments, tips or separate promotion campaigns are **not decided (non deciso)** unless their own rules are announced.

## Variable $GARLY distributions

There is no fixed conversion such as “1 XP = X $GARLY.” The documented weekly programme budget is 5% of the vault, capped by the rule retaining at least 50% of cumulative city-title and Ambassador Premium buy-ins. This programme accounting reserve is not an LP lock or a team vesting contract.

The documented allocation uses work multiplied by tenure. Work includes active citizens, danger reporters, twice the Ambassador's weekly mission XP, citizen weekly XP and invitee weekly XP. The first seven days have zero tenure weight. An idle city does not earn a share merely because its title was purchased.

The documented process produces a payout file for an operator to execute. It is not a promise of instant token transfer after a mission or a fixed weekly income. An Ambassador may share up to 50% of their slice with eligible active citizens under the programme rules; the default share is zero.

## Whistle tickets

Whistle is an external service presented inside City Studio: **own an AI that has on-chain context**. Whistle manages its own sign-in, optional in-app wallet and credit balance. An external wallet connection or a deposit is not needed just to redeem a ticket.

The agreed gift for eligible existing and new Ambassadors is **2,000 Whistle credits**, not $GARLY, cash or a wallet deposit:

1. Garly checks the account's programme entitlement and prepares its ticket.
2. Retrying the same entitlement retrieves the same card, not a second award.
3. Open the ticket in the embedded Whistle experience and sign into the intended Whistle account. Authentication may use a popup while the surrounding Garly page stays in place.
4. Whistle redeems the code and adds 2,000 credits to that account's existing balance. Reopening the same redeemed card must not add them again.

An **unused card lasts 30 days** under the agreed configuration. Whistle applies expiry and its expiry timestamp is authoritative. This redemption window does not establish a 30-day expiry for credits already redeemed.

Do not announce a successful credit while the card is still issued or awaiting confirmation. A refresh is not proof of a second award. Issuance and redemption depend on the active offer, eligibility and Whistle service availability.
